## TLDR

- Finished installing upholstery panels under the throttle quadrant

## Details

Today there is just a bit of wrap-up work remaining for the center channel area.

I crawled under the dashboard to attach the M4 screws for the panels near the pilot/copilot's foot. Then I installed the pilot seat back.

The last pieces to install were the leather cover around the control sticks, which was pretty easy. I just put them on like socks around the two control sticks. They fit perfectly.
