## Details

A couple of weeks ago I called Precision Static Testing to schedule my transponder test and pitot/static check.

Today the technician came, and did both tests. The airplane has no leaks in air hoses, and passed the tests quickly.

While testing the pitot static system, the technician JR also calibrated my G5 so it shows the same altitude as the G3X. Perfect.

Now I can legally fly this airplane!
