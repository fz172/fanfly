## TLDR

- Installed Parachute Rocket

## Details

I installed the rocket for my Magnum 901 parachute. The manual came with my parachute had zero details on how to install the rocket, so I followed [this youtube video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VKHI79-ForE&t=714s) for the most part.

### Steps

1. Remove the rocket from the cage.

It's secured in a metal cage through 2 screws. Once the screws are removed the rocket falls right out.

![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/pw/AP1GczOqV01Cj1XEOfqmsiFRTNiJeiaAc-v6ig4Vg7cdljPPOiJzrkgwodxy4nlm3bkejgBDq1jpXymMv66RHpblLuBVBqdUgg858E7P43fDDPxyy6lXjN879oE1HNQ-VBg2FRMj94oM0pa1ER5cRQlVtydrUQ=w2798-h2098-s-no-gm?authuser=0)

2. Attach the rocket cable.

![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/pw/AP1GczOVfskBSg7YDZDszZPOXHBCNlmi8erOPKnsDVFIB5-O4R1On-w6MUMKf2DtRMnFKS9PDWszalyVUVzDntXzvp4yMMVm3RjrP6kx4Hw-1BvWZsaq18dv7rJDO-oq0gzavzSVQiFArpdCvL-qSC75tEprqg=w2798-h2098-s-no-gm?authuser=0)

3. Arm the striker pins (there are 2 of them)

I forgot to take picture at this step. I have vieo footage though. I will include them when I upload my next video blog.

4. Assemble the trigger

![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/pw/AP1GczOrRLglmdnX2Wh3R6FXt-uAHjzEqSZY9hFAVQh5hedOAJ43cmvkDhK3cnP8HX8gQHFw7Ux7_LLngyPJ5KEE12uNSEBb1JLBJ2o1Lc5N-hJRy30xUfoFlUTYFenu6b4HvPQOFdNK9uFT_HZXGj6iZrOX1A=w1574-h2098-s-no-gm?authuser=0)

5. Insert the armed rocket to the rocket tube

![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/pw/AP1GczOUbrWZHgWo1UPMPXR-PxpssGQCafkLdfXFWmW-DPlOVbP9OLjMNWIpDFe02HS3Sk9izcEXpHWViWof3YcgOXz5TBzfV320Fx9ECPVRjQmc9o7HhMla5Xy_nxPdA0V795mc73229-aLnpGDUlQVaSm-sg=w2798-h2098-s-no-gm?authuser=0)
