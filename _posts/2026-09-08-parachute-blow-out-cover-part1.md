## TLDR

- Match Drilled Parachute Blow-out Cover

## Details

Test fitted the parachute blow-out cover before installing the rocket.

With the cover plate laid out, I used duct-tape to hold it in place. Then I match drilled all the holes around the cover skin and cleco-ed them with 3.2mm clecos.

![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/pw/AP1GczNc4vI4VealaP063oJgMkDo59sP1VjoPRyVwCsPcoEafWYJ3cR8wGYKLCUIysd8c1LNu0xT08UlZcGhc3RbyWESKaP2iPCaMTETD0N6DDM1b-0UwfrkHJUI7k8z5lFPIZ5ZtCclG-FU1HnVv5osWO8vwg=w2976-h2232-s-no-gm?authuser=0)

One hole was right above the parachute cable (the center hole at the front as seen from the pic above). So I drilled a new hole right next to it to clear the cable but maintain the hold-on strength.
