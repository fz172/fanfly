## TLDR

## Details

Today's still preparing for the engine start.

### Fuel Pressure Test

I connected the fuel line to the engine inlet. The full fuel circult is complete.

Then I turned on the Rotax pumps to see the fuel pressure. It reads 46.1/46.2 PSI, which is slightly higher than the 46 PSI normal operation range but smaller than the max range. I am not sure what to think of that.

### Seat Adjustment

Well to start the engine, I need to sit in the airplane. I test fitted both front seats. The pilot seat slides back and forth ok but the copilot seat was properly stuck. I took it back out and ground off a small chunk of the locking latch. It worked much better after the grinding.

### Tail Fairing

I didn't really want to start the engine today. So just picked up some non-urgent task. I started to drill holes to secure the tail fairing.

The fairing fitted very well to the tail right out of the box. I didn't need to do any trimming or sanding. So I just followed the manual instruction to drill holes. Not finished yet, will probably continue after the engine start test.
