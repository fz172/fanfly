## TLDR

- Adjusted the prop fine pitchstop

## Details

I need to adjust the prop fine pitchstop before first flight to ensure the RPM is not over 5800 RPM at full throttle.

I fired up the engine and waited for the oil temp etc to warm up, then taxiied to the run-up area to do this.

The engine was cranked to full throttle, while I kept the prop pitch at manual. Then as the RPM stablized, I used the manual adjust switch in the cockpit to move the blade pitch to finer.

Once I reached 5700 RPM I started moving the blade angle.

Then I taxiied back to the hangar and removed the prop hub to expose the pitchstop adjusment screws.

Based on the service manual from Airmaster, I turned the adjustment screw between blade #1 and #3 counter-clockwise to reduce the fine pitch RPM. I was in a very quite hangar so I was actually able to hear when the pitchstop microstop switch touched the screw. That's the spot I wanted to set.

Once the correct screw position is adjusted, I tightened the jam nut and put the prop hub back on. Then I safety-wired the screws holding the hub, and installed the spinner too. The prop was then ready for dynamic balancing which will hopefully happen next week when Brian has time.

### Another oil leak

As I was working on the prop, I discovered a drip of oil hanging under the right hand side AN8 fitting at the oil pump inlet.

This is extremely annoying. Since it's on the suction side so it's a big deal. I don't want leak, and I absolutely don't want air to be sucked in from the leak. So I will have to come up with something to stop this leak... To be continued I guess.
