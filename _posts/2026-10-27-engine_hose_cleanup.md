### Wire Clean Up

In the engine compartment, a few people in the airport came by and offered their suggestions. One piece of advice I got is that I should consider putting silicone tape on the engine mount when it contacts zip-ties.

So I started to redo zip-ties slowly. It's not done yet, but I ran out of aviation grade zip-ties. I will resume the work once the purchase order arrives.
