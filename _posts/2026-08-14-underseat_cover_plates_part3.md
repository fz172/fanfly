- Installed the pilot side underseat cover plate
- Installed side panels
- Secured the throttle quadrant to the fuel selector

## Details

I've been putting off a few engine- and avionics-related tasks — mainly testing fuel flow and making the first engine start.

I decided to take on something lighter today, so I kept working on the upholstery after finishing the pilot side underseat cover plate installation.

### Throttle Quadrant

First I had to drill holes in the fuel selector for the rivnuts that secure the throttle quadrant.

After that, I slightly enlarged the screw holes on the throttle quadrant for a better fit. The screws then went in pretty easily.
